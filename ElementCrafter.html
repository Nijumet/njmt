How to play

Instructions

  • Tap an element in the list at the bottom to place it on the board.
  • Drag one element onto another to combine them. If the pair has a recipe, they merge into something new.
  • Drag an element onto the list to remove it from the board. Clear board removes everything on the board; Reset erases all your progress.
  • 💡 Hint works in two steps. Press once: one element glows and you get the first letters of what it makes. Press again: its partner appears too. The number shows how many undiscovered elements are within reach. If everything in reach is found, it points you to a recipe you haven't tried yet.

What the colors mean

  • Glowing yellow: a hinted pair. Combine them to make something new.
  • Shaking red: these two don't combine into anything.
  • Purple glow: this element has no recipes with other elements. It can only be upgraded by combining two of the same.
  • Endless upgrades: if an element has no recipes at all, combine two of the same to make a Greater version. Keep going through Mighty, Legendary, Divine, Cosmic and Eternal, then Eternal II, III and beyond. Two Eternal elements with the same name add their numbers, so IV + V makes IX.
  • Yellow in the list: an element you just discovered.
