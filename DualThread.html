DUAL THREAD

Two little robots. One glowing laser tether. Neither can stray too far from the other.

Anchor is heavy. Bolt is light. When the tether runs over a pulley, the heavy one wins the tug-of-war.

Level select:

Solo: WASD / arrows to move & jump, Tab to switch robot.
Co-op: Anchor = WASD, Bolt = arrow keys.
R restarts the level, N / P skip to the next / previous level, Esc returns here.
